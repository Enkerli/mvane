# User test, 2026-11-23: a synthetic user, the 0.15 manual, 0.16's firmware in the simulator

The tester: an agent told to know only [README.md](README.md) and `docs/MANUAL.md` as the Hub linked it (0.15's,
before this day's rewrite), never the source, working the panel in the host simulator and reading its screenshots.
All 12 tasks done, in 27 tries: tasks 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 12 at the first; 2, 4, 5, 10 at the second; 9 at the third;
11 at the fourth (the simulator's encoder is `PRESET`, the README said PRESETS; then finding CLAV).

## Findings, the most important first

1. **A minor scale, major chords** (task 5). SCALE MINOR alone played Cmaj7 Fmaj7 Gmaj7: the qualities bank is the
   default and M7 is latched, and a quality is the same whatever the scale. Minor chords came with QUANT FULL
   (Cm7 Fm7 Gm7), or would with BANK: SCALE. The status line read "C MINOR M7" over a Cm7. The manual never said that
   the first white key (F3) is the tonic.
2. **The slot after the recording** (task 9). Recorded, then moved to slot 3, as most would: "NOT SAVED AGAIN: LOSE
   IT", and the recording was dropped. The manual did not say to pick the slot first. A fresh REC tap got the phrase
   back from the history, so "LOSE IT" overstates it.
3. **The layers table broken**: ONSET's and LEVEL's paragraphs inside it, the GLO rows outside it.
4. **Contradictions**: the screen's knob row "tone, release, glide, attack" (the PLAY page shows QUANT KEY SCALE
   GLIDE); YARD "13 sounds" and "YARD's nine"; CLAV and HOLLOW in no family (CLAV is in HITS EZ); "Scale bank (ENV)"
   and "voices (LFO)" (neither button does anything); "No arpeggiator" under Known limits; PENTA and OCTATONIC, not
   the list's names.
5. **No defaults given**: USB AUDIO starts at FULL (task 3 needed nothing), ARP RATE at 1/16; the boot settings
   nowhere.
6. **The panel**: labels that collide or are cut ("PHONES MAXSPREAD", "ON/OF", "SAVE: KEEP THE SOUNI"); PLAY wraps
   around but USB AUDIO stops at its end; SELECT on EDIT changes the chord type while you only look around; HOME in
   GLO switches the knob page too; a saved CLAV is named USER 1; labels stay lit white after a layer closes; the slot
   grid does not show which slots hold a recording.
7. **The test's README** named the encoder PRESETS where the simulator wants PRESET.

**What went well**: the SETTINGS, EDIT, SEQ and SEL screens explain their own way around (for example "HOME: PHONES
MAX, SPREAD"); tasks 7, 8 and 12 at the first try.

## The three that would help a newcomer most

1. A panel map, and a "first five minutes" box: the boot defaults, the first white key as the tonic, QUANT FULL (or
   BANK: SCALE) for chords from the scale, the slot before the recording.
2. The tables repaired, one row per layer and page, the stale lines gone.
3. Where each YARD sound lives, and the order SELECT steps through the families.
