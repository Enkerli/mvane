# mvane yard's sounds, by family

Generated by `make clp-bank` (tools/clp_bank.c): the order PRESETS runs through. A fast turn of PRESETS, or SELECT on the play pages, goes to the next family. **EZ** and **HRD** say the load on the FM-1's two cores with a chord of six (`make clp-cost`: EZ, about 40% or less), not the sound's size. In each family YARD's own sounds come first (mvane yard's synth: the lightest), then yours (FX and SAVE), then Clipper's, by Clipper's kind. Families by how each sound behaves (held 2.5 s: does it sustain? poly or mono?) and Clipper's own kind where it is clear.
