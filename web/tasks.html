mvane yard: try it

A way to learn the panel, or to test it: do each task on the FM-1, then open its answer. Your ticks stay in this browser.

  1. The answer to task 1

    POLY+BREATH (a wind controller): PLAY page (HOME unlit) - tap GLO - turn K1 to POLY+BR - tap GLO

  2. The answer to task 2

    USB audio at full level: PLAY page - tap GLO - turn K2 to FULL - tap GLO

  3. The answer to task 3

    Headphones quieter at the top: HOME to SOUND (lit) - tap GLO (MORE) - turn K1 (PHONES MAX)

  4. The answer to task 4

    A Diosynth on the USB port: GLO - turn SELECT right (left: back to the computer)

  5. The answer to task 5

    Chords from a minor scale: PLAY page - K3 SCALE to MINOR - K1 QUANT to FULL (or EDIT, K4 BANK to SCALE) - the 1st, 4th, 5th white keys

  6. The answer to task 6

    Two qualities at once: press the M7 and S4 black keys together (they latch together)

  7. The answer to task 7

    A scale by its number: tap SEL - K1 to K4 the digits (2741 C major, 1723 PCO) - SAVE keeps it in your list

  8. The answer to task 8

    Keep what you just played: tap SEQ, SELECT to 3, tap SEQ - play - tap REC (held: the whole history) - SAVE

  9. The answer to task 9

    Loop and overdub: tap SEQ - K2 LOOP ON - tap SEQ - PLAY - tap REC: overdub from now, a layer each pass; hold REC: undo

  10. The answer to task 10

    The arpeggiator: tap ARP - K2 RATE to 1/16 - SELECT, K2 LATCH ON - PLAY (the ARP on) - tap ARP

  11. The answer to task 11

    Edit and keep a sound: PRESETS to CLAV - HOME to SOUND - tap EDIT - SELECT to page 3/3 - K3 LEVEL down 6 steps - SAVE - tap EDIT

  12. The answer to task 12

    Everything off: OCT- and OCT+ together
