Takes

No take saved in this slot yet: on the FM-1, SEQ or HOME to it, REC, then SAVE.

The FM-1 keeps eight takes: SEQ and HOME pick the slot, SAVE keeps the take there. A MIDI file sent to a slot becomes the take PLAY plays in it: its notes, controllers, pressure and bend, all tracks merged (30,000 events at most). Download as MIDI puts everything on one channel, so a DAW makes one clip of it: each note's pressure as poly aftertouch (per-note pressure in Bitwig). MIDI with MPE channels keeps a channel a note, as played, which a DAW may split into a clip a channel.
